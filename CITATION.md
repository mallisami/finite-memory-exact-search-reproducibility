# How to cite or reference this reproducibility repository

**Study title:** *Finite-Memory Retention of Interrupted Exact-Search Checkpoints: Predictive Selection with Compatibility-Gated Reuse.*

**Code/evidence repository:** https://github.com/mallisami/finite-memory-exact-search-reproducibility

The manuscript currently identifies itself as **Anonymous submission**, while this repository URL can disclose the owner. Follow the journal's peer-review policy before inserting this URL in a blinded manuscript or reviewer correspondence.

For a technical review record, identify the **exact full Git commit SHA**, repository URL, access date, and the title above. The code/evidence citation is **not** a substitute for an eventual peer-reviewed article citation or for an author-approved permanent software/data archive.

A formal `CITATION.cff` and DOI are **not yet asserted**: the final author list, venue anonymity decision, license and archival/version identifiers require author/editor confirmation. Do not invent a DOI, publication date or author list to complete bibliographic metadata.

The public repository is the curated reviewer code/evidence subset. The full November 7 author-side manuscript/reproducibility archive is maintained separately; see [editor handoff](docs/EDITOR_SUBMISSION_GUIDE.md) and [provenance boundaries](docs/REVIEWER_EVIDENCE_SOURCE_OF_TRUTH_20261107.md).
